    Why does Hill Climbing get stuck?

    Hill Climbing only looks at nearby positions and moves to the best one if it is strictly better. On a landscape with many peaks, an attempt can reach the top of a small hill where every neighbor is lower. That is a local maximum, and the algorithm stops even though a higher peak exists elsewhere.

    Random restarts repeat the climb from different random starting points, so different attempts can end on different peaks. We keep the best result. This improves the chance of finding a better solution, but it does not guarantee the global maximum.
